# Board 01 — The Place

## Purpose
Capture the felt sense of Benson Stone as a physical destination, not a store. The hearth is where home happens; the building should feel like a quiet, generous host. This board grounds every later screen in the right emotional register.

## What images to include
- Exterior of Benson Stone (early morning light, golden hour, soft weather)
- Showroom interiors: stone walls, wood beams, long sightlines, natural light pools
- Rockford river/landscape context — stone and water belong together
- Architectural details: doorways, thresholds, hand-built textures
- Quiet customer moments: someone touching a sample, looking up at a display
- Detail shots of natural materials in context (stone next to wood, brass, leather)

## What Claude Design should learn
- Warmth without sweetness — weathered, lived-in, generous
- Material truth — real grain, real stone, real shadow
- Spatial calm — large negative space, slow pacing, room to breathe
- Light as a character — directional, warm, never flat or fluorescent
- The brand is a place first and a product second

## What Claude Design should avoid
- Polished retail showroom clichés (white grids, spotlights, glossy floors)
- Big-box hardware energy
- HDR-overcooked / clarity-slider real-estate aesthetics
- Stock-photo "happy family" staging
- Modern startup minimalism — bare white with one accent color
- Any imagery that reads as catalog or e-commerce

## Suggested file naming convention
```
01-place__<location-or-subject>__<lighting>__<id>.<ext>
```
Examples:
- `01-place__exterior-front__golden-hour__001.jpg`
- `01-place__showroom-stone-wall__north-window__003.jpg`
- `01-place__detail-handrail-oak__overcast__002.jpg`

## Notes for Drew while gathering images
- Shoot or pull mid-morning and late-afternoon light. Avoid mid-day flatness.
- Phone is fine. Composition beats megapixels.
- Lean into the corners no one photographs — back hallways, sample stacks, dust on a beam.
- Include at least one image with a human in it but not posed (back of head, hands only, silhouette).
- Save originals; don't pre-edit. We want truth, not glamour.
- If an image makes you feel "this is the place," keep it. If you have to argue for it, don't.
