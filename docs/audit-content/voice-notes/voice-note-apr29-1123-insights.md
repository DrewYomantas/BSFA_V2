## 5. Product specs / technical anatomy

### The Big Taxonomy (the canonical map Drew teaches)

```
FIREPLACES
├── WOOD-BURNING (uses "chimney" — never "vent")
│   ├── Open-face
│   │   ├── Masonry (clay flue tile, old homes, OR IsoKern/Mason-Lite kits — mostly outdoor)
│   │   └── Zero Clearance / ZC (metal box, wood framing can touch)
│   ├── High-efficiency (sealed gasketed glass, doors-closed only, fan kit standard)
│   └── Hybrid (Stuv / Renaissance — guillotine door, both modes)
└── GAS (uses "venting" — never "chimney")
    ├── Direct-vent fireplaces
    │   ├── Traditional (boxy)
    │   ├── Linear
    │   └── Luxury / design-only (low or no heat)
    ├── Direct-vent inserts (slide into existing open-face fireplace)
    ├── Gas log sets
    │   ├── Vented standard (no heat — burns like wood)
    │   ├── Vented radiant heat (BENSON STOCK / DEFAULT)
    │   └── Vent-free (Rockford requires open damper)
    └── Direct-vent stoves (free-standing)

STOVES (free-standing)
├── Wood-burning (must end vertically, can elbow horizontally first)
├── Gas direct-vent (usually horizontal exit)
└── Vent-free (banned in Rockford & Wisconsin)

INSERTS
├── Gas (most common — into open-face)
└── Wood-burning (masonry only — NOT approved for ZC)
```

### Wood-burning fireplaces

- **Open-face primary function:** **Ambiance only.** "If anything... it just pulls such a draft from the house that makes the house colder."
- **Open-face venting:** Vendor-specific chimney (Heatilator sells its own). Masonry = clay flue tile.
- **High-efficiency primary function:** **Heat.** Floor model heats **3,000 sqft.** Most installed units are **2,000–2,500 sqft.**
- **High-efficiency MUST be burned doors-closed** (backdraft risk + voids manual compliance).
- **Outside air = required** for all wood-burning (open-face AND high-efficiency). "If you don't get outside air, you will not have a draft. Majority of the units require them."
- **Fan kit:** Standard on every high-efficiency wood burner. **Not worth adding to open-face** ("just blowing air, not heated air — no convection system").
- **Stuv hybrid:** Only wood-burner that can run doors-open. Heats 1,100 sqft → closer to high-efficiency than open-face. "Crazy expensive."
- **Wood-burning chimney = ALWAYS vertical termination.** Cannot end horizontally. Can elbow horizontally first, then must turn vertical.

### Masonry conversion rules

- Masonry open-face can be converted to gas (insert or log set or log lighter).
- ZC open-face can be converted to gas — **but conversion voids the wood-burning use forever** (yellow tag from installer).

### Gas direct-vent inserts

- **Sealed gasketed glass box** that slides into existing open-face opening.
- **Liner** runs from chimney top down to top of insert.
- **Vent pipe spec:** 3"x3" exhaust OR 3"x4" exhaust depending on unit size (one intake, one exhaust).
- **Does NOT pull room air for combustion.** Pulls room air for convection only.
- **Faceplate** silicone-sealed or screwed to facing material — last component installed.
- **Customer chooses:** faceplate, surround panel, background liner, logs.
- **Solves drafting issues** that open-face + log set cannot fix.

### Gas log sets

- **#1 money maker.** "Important to key in on these because that's what you're going to be doing."
- **No site measure required** — Drew uses **Hargrove's online calculator** with customer's dimensions.
- **Components:** burner + grate + logs + valve (+ optional remote).
- **Vented standard:** Burns like wood — damper must be open, doors must be open — **almost no heat.** Benson does NOT stock standard sets.
- **Vented radiant heat:** **BENSON STOCK.** Pushes ~15 ft of livable heat. "Livable heat — heats objects and skin, not house/room."
- **Vent-free:** Operated/installed same as vented — but designed for damper-closed (max heat). Cannot be flame-touching-logs (sooting issue), so **flame appearance is uglier than vented.** Slight gas odor when burning. **Will increase indoor moisture levels** ("you're getting all the effects of combustion").
- **Rockford rule:** Vent-free **logs** are legal in Rockford if there's a working damper (open the damper when using).

### Gas direct-vent fireplaces

- Same sealed-glass system as direct-vent inserts, but standalone unit.
- **Can vent horizontally OR vertically** (unlike wood, which is vertical-only).
- **Most Benson installs are horizontal** ("saves on costs, save on install").
- **Framing required** + gas + electric.
- **Most include fan kit standard** — some it's an option.
- **Linear vs Traditional** = aspect ratio terminology (linear = wide rectangle, traditional = boxy).
- **Luxury / design-only** = high-end with little/no heat output.
- **General mantel clearance rule of thumb: 12 inches** (always verify manual — varies by brand, e.g., 10" or 12").
- **Cool-wall system NOT needed by default** — fireplace usually low enough that standard mantel clearance + TV placement works. Cool-wall only needed if customer wants raised unit on 8-ft ceiling.

### Ignition systems (MV vs IPI)

| Spec | **Millivolt (MV)** | **IPI (Intermittent Pilot Ignition)** |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Standing pilot, burns continuously | No standing pilot — sparks on demand |
| Electricity | **Not needed** (thermopile self-powers valve) | **Required** |
| Gas usage when off | "$1–$2 a month" pilot consumption | Zero |
| Power outage | Works normally | Battery backup (hard to access) or home generator |
| Cost | Cheaper | More expensive (upsell) |
| Safety | Standard | "Safer" per Drew |
| Future | **Being phased out** | **Industry default direction** |

- **NEW CODE — Rockford banning millivolt** within city limits; Winnebago County expected to follow. **All future Rockford sales must be IPI.**
- **Brand exposure:** Only **Kingsman** still ships with millivolt option among brands Benson carries. Every other brand is IPI-only or IPI-default.
- **No retrofit possible:** "If someone has a millivolt fireplace, the only way they're getting an IPI fireplace is if they switch out the whole fireplace." Burner + valve + logs are integrated.

### Stoves

- **Wood-burning stoves:** Same behavior as high-efficiency wood-burning fireplace. North-south (logs vertical, taller rectangle) vs east-west (logs horizontal, wider rectangle) loading. Black pipe transitions to stainless steel chimney; must end vertically; elbows allowed for horizontal run first. **Fans = add-on, not standard.** **Outside air strongly recommended but not required** — Benson does more wood stoves WITHOUT outside air than with.
- **Gas direct-vent stoves:** Mostly horizontal venting. Floor model heats **1,500 sqft.**
- **Vent-free stoves:** Exist but **illegal in Rockford and Wisconsin.** Benson doesn't stock display. Customers ask for them for basements — Drew steers away: "There's not a lot of airflow down there. The smell is going to be real."

### Wood-burning inserts

- **Masonry-only.** Explicitly: "**Not approved for zero-clearance installation.**"
- "If you pull this out and put legs on it, it's a wood-burning stove" — mechanically same unit.
- New construction = customer would buy a high-efficiency wood fireplace, not an insert. Inserts are **retrofit-only.**

### Fuel type

- **Always ask: natural gas or propane?**
- Most Benson sales = natural gas. **Dixon-area homes are frequently propane.**
- Conversion kit OR factory-propane SKU available.
- "Propane burns a little bit more dirty."

## 6. Sales techniques / patterns

1. **Discovery-question-first for converters:** Never assume what the open-face-to-gas customer wants. "Heat? Ambiance? Both?" answers the SKU category.
2. **Correct customer vocabulary firmly:** "Insert" misuse is rampant; correct it courteously. "It may seem like you're being annoying but you're actually helping them."
3. **Downsell for fit:** Drew is willing to take the lower sale to make a happier customer. Oversizing high-efficiency wood units is the most common error.
4. **Lead with safety/health caveats on vent-free** — proactively disclose smell + COPD risk before customer buys, not after. The "step close and breathe in" demo is a trust-building proprietary script.
5. **Use square footage, not BTUs, as the customer frame.** Customers who arrive citing online BTU recommendations are almost always wrong.
6. **Reframe IPI power-outage objection** by anchoring on home generator (most premium-fireplace customers have one).
7. **Stuv as back-pocket upsell:** Don't lead with it; bring it out only when customer signals specialty/uniqueness preference.
8. **ZC-conversion permanent-alteration disclosure** is mandatory — past sales reps who omitted it created costly situations.
9. **Customer-facing language tier:** Use "livable heat" instead of "radiant heat" for non-technical customers.
10. **Outdoor masonry kits (IsoKern/Mason-Lite) are an undersold opportunity** — drew implies they're more popular for outdoor than indoor and easier to sell than full mason builds.

## 10. Customer-types covered

This transcript is organized by **customer scenario**, not customer demographic. Scenarios Drew explicitly walks through:

1. **"I have an open-face wood fireplace and I'm tired of burning wood"** — **70% of all incoming customers** by Drew's own estimate. Decision tree: heat → insert; ambiance only → log set; both → log set (radiant).
2. **"I want a wood-burning unit that heats my house"** — direct to high-efficiency wood. Right-size warning if home is small.
3. **"I want a fireplace but I don't want to feel any drafts"** — direct-vent insert is the answer.
4. **"I want just a little ambiance and a little heat, nothing complicated"** — radiant heat vented gas log set.
5. **"I want max heat and don't care about anything else"** — vent-free log set (with safety disclosure).
6. **"I'm building a new home — what do I do?"** — high-efficiency wood burner or direct-vent gas fireplace. NOT a wood-burning insert (those are retrofit-only).
7. **"My existing 20-year-old direct-vent fireplace died, parts unavailable"** — replacement is whatever fits the opening. "Customer isn't really choosing... at the mercy of what fireplace fits."
8. **"I want a fireplace in my workshop/basement"** — gas stove direction. Vent-free stove is what they ask for, but Drew steers away (airflow + smell).
9. **"Dixon-area customer"** — check fuel type; many are propane, not natural gas.
10. **"Someone in home has COPD/asthma/breathing issues"** — **no vent-free recommendation, ever.**
11. **"Power-outage concerned customer"** — IPI objection handling via home-generator anchor.
