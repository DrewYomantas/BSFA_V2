## 5. Product specs / anatomy

### Outdoor fire-pit media (Fire Gear)

- **Reflective glass:** shiny, flat-edged pieces. Catches light. ($49 / 10 lb)
- **Broken glass:** matte/dull, no flat edges. Cheaper. ($39 / 10 lb)
- **Lava rock:** dark charcoal/black, jagged surface — best base material because jaggedness props up glass. ($29 / 10 lb)
- **Lava stones:** light gray, smooth — alternative base but you need more because smoothness lets glass fall through. (no price captured)
- **Recommended build:** lava rock base + glass top layer. Saves money vs. all-glass and looks cleaner because lava jagged surface holds the glass up.
- **Sizing chart on Fire Gear brochure:** square fire pits have a coverage table (e.g., 30"×30" square pit needs ~50 lb or ~90 lb depending on glass-only vs glass-on-base). Customer's pit was estimated as **30" or 30"–38" square**.

### Stone veneer (Prestige Harvest Weather Ledge specifically)

- **Flats coverage:** **13.5 sqft per box** at $104/box
- **Corners:** sold by **linear foot**, not square foot
- **Square-foot-to-linear-foot conversion (repeated from Apr 30):** **each 1 linear ft of corner = 0.75 sqft of flat coverage** — deduct that much from flat order. Drew explicitly re-teaches this rule to the trainee ("I know I've told you this before, but you need to hear it over and over").
- **Flat definition:** "a stone that is veneered into a flat piece... not a full-size stone. It's a thin veneer." Can be **natural stone, thin brick, or manufactured stone** (Prestige = manufactured).
- **Corner definition:** "90-degree corner pieces... made for when you're doing a buildout and you're going up. That way you're not putting two flats together and you see the edge there."

### Custom Limestone (Indiana Buff)

- **Item code in BisTrack:** every Limestone product is under the `Hearth` item code. Description gets rewritten manually per piece.
- **Standard thickness for hearth/mantel:** **2.25 inches** (two and a quarter inches)
- **Standard finish on visible edges:** **Rock Face 3 sides**
- **Pricing sheet location:** "Limestone Hearth pricing sheet... top left corner Limestone two and a quarter inches thick, 48 a square foot"
- **Same-item-code dialog warning:** When adding a second line with the same item code as an existing line, BisTrack asks "Would you like to change the price in these lines also? **Say NO.**" That way the second line gets independent pricing + notes.

- Existing: portable fireplace with drywall, rear-opening
- Wants to keep drywall (no cool-wall kit) → must use **non-combustible mantel** (= Limestone)
- Wants TV above unit
- Won't lower the unit

7. **"If it were my fire pit"** — first-person recommendation anchor. Stronger than "people usually do X."
8. **Honest about competitor workarounds** — openly told the walk-in that Hernandez Brothers buys glass from a box store. Doesn't hide it, doesn't try to lock her in. Trust play.
9. **Re-teach the linear-ft-to-sqft conversion every time** — "I know I've told you this before, but you need to hear it over and over." Repetition is a deliberate training tool.
10. **Turn problems into training opportunities** — James's log-set call became a "Drew field trip" with the trainee. Site visit > continued quote work when there's a live install issue to learn from.
