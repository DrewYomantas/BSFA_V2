## Apr 29 at 12:31 PM — Electric fireplace walk-in (pellet stove removal) (12 KB)

**Context:** Walk-in couple. Have a noisy pellet stove insert (built into a new house ~20 years ago); want to remove it and install electric for ambiance. Getting the house re-sided (hail damage).

**Key insights:**
- Pellet stove insert vents horizontally out the side wall (a "bump-out").
- Customer plan: siding crew cuts off the bump-out, frames the hole, sides over it; inside gets drywalled. Then a plain wall + electric fireplace.
- **Electric depth rule (canonical):** nearly ALL electric units need **10-12" depth** and must be framed-in. **Only ONE wall-mount model exists that's 4" deep** (partially built-in) and can simply hang on the wall. This is a recurring constraint — capture in BSFA.
- **Electric specs:** ~5,000 BTU heater "across the board"; 110V plug-and-play; no venting, no clearances; changeable flame + amber-bed colors.
- **Electric lead time:** "1-2 weeks, but typically once we order — about a day or two — they show up." Fast.
- Sizes: traditional/shake shapes are in the cellar; linear shapes on the main floor. 60" wide unit referenced at ~$1,699.
- **À la carte project scope:** "We can do as much or as little — sell you the box, or do the full install run."
- **The hard part = pellet stove removal.** Customer doesn't know how it was installed. Recurring friction: old appliance removal is its own mini-project.

**Sales-pattern takeaway:** Jonathan's qualifying question again — "are you looking to get heat from it, or just to sell the face?" Electric = ambiance/face, not heat. Confirm before quoting.
